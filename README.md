# Global-Restaurant-Performance-Star-Restaurant-Analysis
This Tableau project explores global restaurant performance, operational models, and consumer engagement using restaurant data (zomato) across 13 worksheets and three interactive dashboards: Restaurant Overview, Rating Influencers, and Star Restaurant Dashboard

## Project Overview and Objectives

This project provides an end-to-end visual exploration of global restaurant performance, operational strategies, and consumer engagement using restaurant data (`zomato`) in Tableau. The objective is to evaluate geographical restaurant distribution, cuisine demand, pricing and rating patterns, service availability (online delivery and table booking), and top-performing "star restaurants" across 13 worksheets and 3 interactive dashboards.

## Technologies and Tools Used

* **Data Visualization & BI:** Tableau Public / Tableau Desktop Public Edition (13 worksheets and 3 dashboards)


* **Data Fields & Dimensions:** Restaurant dataset (`zomato`) with fields including Restaurant Name/ID, City, Country, Cuisines (split into `Cuisines - Split 1` through `Cuisines - Split 6`), `Num of cuisines`, `Average Cost for two`, `Price range`, `Aggregate rating`, `Votes`, `Has Online delivery`, `Has Table booking`, and geographic coordinates (`Latitude`, `Longitude`)



## Key Features and Functionalities

* **Market & Operational Views:** Tracks distinct restaurant counts by city and country, franchise presence across countries, table booking ratios, online delivery availability, and vote counts by delivery option.


* **Culinary & Cost Analysis:** Highlights top cuisines from split cuisine fields (led by North Indian with 2,992, Chinese with 855, and Fast Food with 672 in the primary cuisine view) alongside cost distribution bins for average cost for two.


* **Rating Influencer Analysis:** Examines aggregate rating distributions, ratings versus average cost for two (colored by online delivery and sized by votes), ratings by online delivery and table booking, and ratings versus number of cuisines (peaking at 2 cuisines).


* **Interactive Dashboards:** Consolidates views into three dashboards—**Restaurant Overview**, **Rating Influencers**, and **Star Restaurant Dashboard** (combining `Star Restaurant` rating vs. votes with online delivery and table booking rating views).
